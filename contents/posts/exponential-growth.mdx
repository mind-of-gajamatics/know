## Amazing Property

The derivative of e^x is... **e^x itself**! This means the rate of growth equals the current value.

## Real World Examples

- 💰 **Compound interest**: Your money grows exponentially
- 🦠 **Population growth**: Bacteria doubling
- ☢️ **Radioactive decay**: Nuclear physics
- 📱 **Technology adoption**: How innovations spread
