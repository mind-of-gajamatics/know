# Introduction to Calculus

Welcome to the world of **calculus**! This is where mathematics meets motion and change.

## What is a Derivative?

A derivative tells us the **rate of change** at any point. Think of it as the slope of a curve!

<MathGraph
  title="f(x) = x²"
  func={(x) => x * x}
  xMin={-5}
  xMax={5}
  color="#3b82f6"
  width={600}
  height={400}
/>

The derivative of x² is 2x, which means the slope increases linearly!

## Key Concepts

- **Instantaneous rate of change**: The slope at a single point
- **Tangent line**: A line that touches the curve at exactly one point
- **Limit**: The foundation of calculus

> "The derivative is the heart of calculus - it captures change in its purest form."
